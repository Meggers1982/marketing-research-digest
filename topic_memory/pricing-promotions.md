# Topic Memory: Pricing & Promotions
_Last updated: 2026-10-27_

## Established findings
- Display, framing, and interface design move demand with no price change: tier-savings salience (W7220990040), filter availability shifting shoppers to pricier, more varied baskets (W7223129134, 2026-10), markdown wording (W7204248241), discount-depth framing at price parity (W7172409631), defaults (W7160239758).
- Stated willingness to pay is a fragile pricing input: weaker than observed spend (W7203715795), misstated by LLM synthetic respondents (W7214644774), and materially dependent on conjoint price specification and budget constraints (W7221780439, 2026-10).
- Subscription value runs on habit and psychology, not discount economics: spending lift persisting after cancellation (W7222938569, 2026-10), cognitive relief over time savings (W7221025098), relational bonds blocking cancellation (W7215055600), immediate activation/ownership (W7170377972).
- Trust damage from pricing runs through perceived deception, not price level: conditional review incentives (W7221330457), drip pricing producing betrayal then anger then dissatisfaction (W7214557291, 2026-09).
- Price response is segmented by disposition as well as income: latent classes with 60% price-insensitive smokers (W7215078694), income-conditioned grocery cutbacks (W7204997399), socially reticent shoppers skewing price-conscious (W7223569994, 2026-10).
- Marketing budget and sponsorship defensibility track financial position and capital-market logic, not campaign performance (W7212389830; W7215085152; event-study reversal in W7212373919, 2026-09).

## Emerging threads
- Friction reduction cuts both ways: removing arithmetic helps conversion (W7220990040) while removing exploration narrows and cheapens choice and raises regret (W7223129134, W7221672134).
- Platforms as price-setters whose "generosity" is financed downstream — influencer tools paid for by higher ad prices (W7220981580), fuzzy segmentation (W4410534098), freemium under adaptive learning (W7213561739).
- Algorithmic attribution as blame absorber: AI-set prices drew weaker betrayal responses than human-set ones (W7214557291) — one study only, worth watching.
- Price formation where no list price exists: collector grading conventions (W7212042826), donation vocabulary in paid spiritual retreats (W7207733471).
- Climate policy as consumer price pass-through, with destination-economy exposure modeled rather than observed (W7223185322).
- Cross-category basket synchrony as a promotion lever, e.g. human wellness and pet aisles (W7221947266).
