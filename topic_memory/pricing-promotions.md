# Topic Memory: Pricing & Promotions
_Last updated: 2026-11-10_

## Established findings
- Display, environment, and interface design move demand with no price change: filter availability (W7223129134), tier-savings salience (W7220990040), markdown wording (W7204248241), and now ambiguous store/site changes that reset price attention among habitual shoppers (W7225627982, 2026-11).
- Stated willingness to pay is a fragile pricing input: weaker than observed spend (W7203715795), distorted by LLM synthetic respondents (W7214644774), dependent on conjoint price specification (W7221780439), and the basis of most sustainability-premium claims (W7225468467, W7221815863, 2026-11).
- Trust damage from pricing and promotion runs through perceived deception or skepticism, not price level: conditional review incentives (W7221330457), drip pricing producing betrayal then anger (W7214557291), and elaborate AI disclosure read as "AI-washing" (W7225591358, 2026-11). Optimum is early, brief, shopper-serving disclosure.
- Price response is segmented by disposition, not just income: price-insensitive smoker latent classes (W7215078694) and dual users who ignore cigarette price entirely (W7225156193, 2026-11); socially reticent shoppers skewing price-conscious (W7223569994).
- Subscription and discount acquisition pay off through post-purchase behavior, not the discount: post-cancellation spending lift (W7222938569), front-loaded deal-cohort cash flows with no erosion of existing-customer value when deals are honored (W7225402212, 2026-11).
- Price formation where no list price exists rests on shared conventions and intermediary anchors: auction pre-sale estimates tracking realized prices (W7224345056, 2026-10), collector grading language (W7212042826), donation vocabulary (W7207733471).

## Emerging threads
- Social proof underperforming: peer signals weakest for green premium (W7221815863), social-norm and recognition nudges null versus confrontational framing (W7225388749) — two studies, watch for replication.
- Platforms as price-setters whose openness decisions reallocate margin downstream: influencer tools financed by ad-price rises (W7220981580), AI-agent access models where platforms may permit reading but block buying (W7224901915).
- Algorithmic pricing shifting from accuracy to auditability: SHAP attribution plus competitor-response simulation (W7167636549) alongside the conjoint-specification warning (W7221780439).
- Behavioral interventions priced as substitutes for cash incentives (W7225388749); situational framing moving voluntary payment under pay-what-you-want (W7213671471).
- Access-market pricing: availability, affordability, store usability, and cultural fit as four separate hurdles (W7225110412).
- Time-to-impact as a persuasion variable in giving, overriding objective effectiveness measures (W7224975922).
