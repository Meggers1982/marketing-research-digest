# Topic Memory: Pricing & Promotions
_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

## Established findings
- Display and framing move demand without any price change: tier-savings salience (W7220990040, 2026-10), markdown date-vs-delay wording (W7204248241), deeper-looking markdown at price parity (W7172409631), defaults (W7160239758).
- Reference prices dominate paid prices; a remembered or displayed "was" price reshapes perceived value even when unusable (W7207565403, 2026-08).
- Stated willingness to pay is an unreliable pricing input — weaker than observed spend (W7203715795), and badly reproduced by LLM synthetic respondents (W7214644774, 2026-09).
- Subscription retention runs on psychological attachment, not value or discounts: immediate activation/ownership (W7170377972), relational bonds blocking cancellation (W7215055600).
- Marketing budget defensibility tracks the firm's financial position, not marketing performance (W7212389830; W7215085152, 2026-10).
- Price response is segmented, not uniform: latent classes with 60% price-insensitive smokers (W7215078694); income-conditioned grocery cutbacks (W7204997399).

## Emerging threads
- Privacy as a pricing currency: discounts offset telematics disutility and disclosure aids adoption (W7214964045); anonymity may attract a price premium via fuzzy segmentation (W4410534098); voice-inferred confidence (W7210283217).
- Generative AI entering price-setting on both sides — synthetic demand estimates (W7214644774) and freemium cannibalization under adaptive learning (W7213561739).
- Conditionality, not incentives, is what breaks trust: review-for-reward wording (W7221330457), alongside earlier backfiring signals (W4375826978, W7170087457).
- Price parity vs promotional parity diverge in omnichannel retention (W7221526089); fairness-of-pricing thread also in regional platform pricing (W7203929361).
- Affordability-only strategies may entrench rather than relieve constrained households (W7204257214, W7204997399).
- Enforcement and distribution, not tax levels, drive illicit-market share in regulated categories (W7215090900).
