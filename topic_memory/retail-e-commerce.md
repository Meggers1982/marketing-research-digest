# Topic Memory: Retail & E-Commerce
_Last updated: 2026-10-14_

## Established findings
- Trust signals work best when third-party or structurally verified, and are attention-gated rather than reasoned: randomly assigned marketplace badges doubled selection only among shoppers who noticed them (W7221510551, 2026-10); earlier runs showed self-issued retailer endorsements and uniformly positive eWOM backfiring (W7203930195, W7171935030, 2026-08).
- Credibility signals substitute rather than compound: online reputation and offline event presence cancel each other out for B2B sellers (W7220830415, 2026-10); sustainability launches lift parent-brand equity only for firms with existing ethical reputations (W7221350449, 2026-10).
- Returners are not a distinct customer type: 8 years of Nordic fashion data found no causal spending effect of returning and near-universal returner status with tenure (W7221059811, 2026-10), consistent with prior findings that discretionary returns precede higher spend (W7208808437) and that abuse tracks peer norms, not policy (W7212072927).
- Live commerce revenue tracks host emotional performance, not chat volume; engagement metrics and sales can diverge (W7220454801, 2026-10), extending the 2026-08/09 finding that charisma follows inverted-U curves against real sales (W7172490860).
- Loyalty and channel investments pay off conditionally on customer disposition and pathway: loyalty engagement requires joint presence of benefits, trust and commitment (W7204202782, 2026-08); acquisition pathway determines online-adopter profitability (W7202042423).

## Emerging threads
- Operational log data is falsifying standard playbook moves — rule-based bots beating LLM bots (W7214950393), AI summaries increasing rather than cannibalizing reviews (W7220470690), paid search feeding later "free" direct/organic traffic (W7220946924). Watch for replication outside single-firm samples.
- AI as interface and as threat simultaneously: avatar anthropomorphism lifts engagement modestly and conditionally (W7215066966) while generative AI scales fraud that mimics legitimate persuasion tactics (W7211892755) — a possible regulatory/brand-authentication storyline.
- Friction as value: collection framing makes search itself pleasurable and raises desirability (W7221306245), cutting against a decade of frictionless-checkout orthodoxy.
- Constrained choice in emerging markets: store-level lock-in via credit and proximity decides brand access before marketing operates (W7214970397); delivery-fee sensitivity is high and heterogeneous in e-grocery (W7204970234).
- Influencer fit is mindset-dependent, not reach-dependent: hedonic shoppers forgive off-domain recommendations, functional shoppers punish them via authenticity (W7221454638).
