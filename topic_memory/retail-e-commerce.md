# Topic Memory: Retail & E-Commerce
_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

## Established findings
- Trust and credibility cues work as attention and reassurance devices rather than reasoned arguments, and substitute rather than compound: verification badges drive selection only when noticed (W7221510551, 2026-10); membership badges raise perceived expertise but cannot offset weak content (W7223652730, 2026-10); online reputation and trade-show presence cancel out (W7220830415, 2026-10); bank branches signal permanence from the outside (W7223672553, 2026-10).
- Technology-forward CX consistently underdelivers against competence and low-effort design: rule-based bots beat LLM bots on live logs (W7214950393), avatar anthropomorphism lifts engagement only modestly and conditionally (W7215066966), smart carts produce fatigue not empowerment (W7223273403), tech-enhanced branches add little beyond a functional baseline (W7223672553).
- Engagement metrics and revenue routinely diverge, and averages hide the real effect: live-stream chat vs sales (W7220454801), teaser-ad completers showing a 46.6% lift inside a flat average (W7223280551, 2026-10), push notifications tipping into fatigue at high frequency (W7223280000, 2026-10).
- Marketing spend generates cross-entity spillover that own-conversion metrics undervalue: paid search feeding later direct/organic traffic (W7220946924), pre-arrival coupons pulling mall traffic that neighbors capture (W7223707755), push for one merchant lifting unadvertised merchants (W7223280000).
- Returners are not a distinct customer type; returner status tracks tenure and the profit lever is reverse-logistics cost (W7221059811, 2026-10, with W7208808437, W7212072927).
- Review ecosystems are shaped by platform design and contributor identity, not just product quality: loyalty members write enthusiastic, low-diagnostic reviews (W7223652730); AI summaries increase volume and shift reviewers toward gap-filling (W7220470690).

## Emerging threads
- Ambient and sensory context as a measurable commercial variable, mismeasured by current instruments: perceived (not sensed) air quality drives dwell and spend (W7222049986), crowding suppresses mobile investing (W7221805592), packaging smile cues inflate calorie estimates for restrained eaters (W7222498555).
- Values and cause signaling inside the servicescape has dose-response risk: political cues stacked across staff and store raise discomfort and cut store choice (W7221815419); local charity partners outperform global ones at checkout (W7223898707).
- Consumer credulity is the baseline, not skepticism: 58.9% accuracy at detecting deceptive claims, worsened by brand presence (W7223392067) — pairs with the AI-fraud framing (W7211892755) and complicates protective regulation.
- Experienced platform users resist consumer-protection rules, making heavy users a regulatory constituency (W7223071223). Watch for replication outside South Korea.
- Channel configuration serves different goals — webrooming for the next transaction, online-to-offline for loyalty (W7220357245) — and voice commerce finds a narrow Gen Z, hedonic, low-involvement niche (W7223671637).
- Friction and absorption beat control: collection framing makes search pleasurable (W7221306245) while added information and choice tire shoppers (W7223273403).
