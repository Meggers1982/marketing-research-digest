# Topic Memory: Retail & E-Commerce
_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

## Established findings
- Consumer suspicion governs persuasion: vague promotions suppress spend (W7203672036, 2026-08); self-issued retailer health endorsements backfire (W7203930195, 2026-08); uniformly positive eWOM lowers credibility (W7171935030, 2026-07); ESG converts only when motive reads as sincere (W7214549693, 2026-09).
- Structural/third-party trust beats displayed or self-issued trust: external credentials close the SES advice gap (W7212164165, 2026-09); authorized-store badges insulate ad returns from bad reviews (W7204166660, 2026-08); eBay's public star ratings hid bad sellers until search was reranked on an inferred quality measure (W3121242579, 2026-08).
- Returns and relationships are not uniform assets: discretionary returns precede higher spend (W7208808437); returns abuse tracks peer norms and status motives, not policy leniency (W7212072927, W7172339845); stable trading relationships predict platform disintermediation (W7214190181, 2026-09).
- Channel and immersion investments pay off conditionally: AR/VR attributes map to distinct funnel stages (W7203672599); acquisition pathway determines online-adopter profitability (W7202042423); pickup lockers lift retention, not acquisition (W7214672185, 2026-09).
- Merchandising is a testable lever with real downside: mixed-body mannequins lift WTP (W7203777252) and AI-redesigned packs win narrowly (W7207745459), but a themed "imported products" endcap cut unit sales 30% by removing comparison context (W7213671071, 2026-09).

## Emerging threads
- Live commerce doesn't transfer: platform beliefs shape response (W7214279733), host type should match product type (W7213554170), and nonverbal charisma follows inverted-U curves against real sales (W7172490860) — all 2026-08/09.
- Physical amenities as retention infrastructure: EV chargers lifting grocery revenue 3.4% (W7213254140), lockers cutting churn (W7214672185), hygiene cues buffering crowding penalties (W7213558307).
- AI interface architecture as persuasion: multi-agent recommendations raise felt autonomy (W7202143753); culturally fluent chatbots raise perceived brand localness (W7214665435).
- Compulsive-consumption design risk in surprise retail formats (W7212380221) — watch for regulatory framing of blind boxes as gambling-adjacent.
- Counter-intuition on decision support: preference uncertainty can raise purchase intent in high-variability categories (W7213439880), complicating the "help shoppers clarify" reflex.
- Unresolved/untested frameworks: cultural-appropriation harm typology (W7202056329), celebrity three-axis fit (W7170143274), product social currency scale (W7214728277).
