# Topic Memory: Retail & E-Commerce
_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

## Established findings
- Technology-forward CX underdelivers against competence, flow and willingness to use: rule-based bots beat LLM bots (W7214950393), smart carts cause fatigue not empowerment (W7223273403), tech-enhanced bank branches add little (W7223672553), AR converts via repeat-use intention and mood rather than features (W7225315487, 2026-11), and digital rollouts stall where HQ and frontline read the same project differently (W7225231563, 2026-11).
- Review and rating ecosystems reflect contributor identity and platform design more than product quality: loyalty members write enthusiastic low-diagnostic reviews (W7223652730), AI summaries shift reviewer behavior (W7220470690), critic acclaim tracks heavy engaged buyers but not market share (W7225744799, 2026-11).
- Engagement metrics and revenue diverge, and averages hide the real effect: teaser-ad completers (W7223280551), push-notification fatigue curves (W7223280000), livestream assertiveness working only at low perceived interactivity (W7224102790, 2026-10).
- Promotion value is mismeasured at the channel level: pre-arrival coupons feed neighboring tenants (W7223707755), push lifts unadvertised merchants (W7223280000), subsidies pay off far more for pickup than home delivery (W7224921670, 2026-10) — though spatial spillover is not universal.
- Values and cause signaling in the servicescape has real, mechanism-driven effects: political cue saturation raises discomfort (W7221815419), local charity partners beat global ones (W7223898707), DEIA awareness tracks trust across seven annual waves (W7225631783, 2026-11), and DEI statement specificity works only by closing psychological distance (W7221486834, 2026-11).
- Consumer credulity is the baseline, not skepticism (W7223392067), and experienced platform users resist protective regulation (W7223071223).

## Emerging threads
- Situational state beats demographic trait as a predictor: generational cohort explains almost nothing about review response (W7225751353, 2026-11), while mood (W7225315487), perceived interactivity (W7224102790), self-presentation mode (W7225714650) and market context (W7223000149) all move behavior.
- Ambient context as a commercial variable mismeasured by current instruments: perceived vs sensed air quality (W7222049986), crowding and mobile investing (W7221805592), packaging smile cues (W7222498555).
- Omnichannel as one capability with a measurable one-year tail on retailer-level satisfaction (W7225122614, 2026-11), building on channel-configuration differences (W7220357245).
- Qualitative work reframing consumer "problems" as functional: household disorder as a working tool (W7222556775), embarrassment as a retail-design failure in taboo categories (W7225430644).
- Platformization of incumbents: B2B marketplace launches split into pipeline-enhancing vs pipeline-transcending paths depending on managerial reference point (W7225100025).
- ESG/sustainability claims tracking public attention rather than impact, creating partner-credibility risk (W7224526178).
