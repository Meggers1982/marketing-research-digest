# Topic Memory: Retail & E-Commerce
_Last updated: 2026-09-16_

## Established findings
- Consumer suspicion is a measurable brake on retail persuasion: vague "up to X" promotions suppress spend among prevention-focused shoppers (W7203672036, 2026-08); self-issued retailer health endorsements reduce perceived healthiness (W7203930195, 2026-08); uniformly positive eWOM lowers credibility and value (W7171935030, 2026-07).
- Returns are not a uniform cost: discretionary returns are followed by higher spending (W7208808437, 2026-09), while returns abuse tracks peer norms, financial pressure and status-driven desire to outsmart firms rather than policy leniency (W7212072927 and W7172339845, 2026-08/09).
- Third-party/structural trust signals outperform brand-issued ones: external credentials close the SES advice-taking gap (W7212164165, 2026-09); platform authorized-store badges insulate ad returns from negative reviews (W7204166660, 2026-08).
- Channel and immersion investments pay off conditionally, not universally: AR/VR attributes map to distinct funnel stages (W7203672599, 2026-08); online-adoption value depends on acquisition pathway, with promo-driven adopters least profitable (W7202042423, 2026-08).

## Emerging threads
- Loyalty and relationship framing as a liability — relationship cues licensing opportunism (W7172339845); stranded loyalty points read as donatable rather than valuable (W7212193195).
- Merchandising and imagery as cheap, testable commercial levers: mixed-body mannequins lifting willingness to pay (W7203777252), AI-redesigned packaging winning real-shelf choice by a narrow margin (W7207745459), visual complexity type (not amount) dampening arousal (W7204047411).
- Unregulated eco-vocabulary carrying specific hidden inferences ("sustainable" implying durability, W7208768688) — watch for follow-ups tied to greenwashing enforcement.
- Ownership and blame allocation in the last mile: outsourcing buffers failure but forfeits upside (W7202034360).
- Frameworks awaiting quantitative tests: cultural-appropriation harm typology (W7202056329), celebrity-brand three-axis fit (W7170143274).
