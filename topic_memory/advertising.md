# Topic Memory: Advertising
_Last updated: 2026-10-23_

## Established findings
- Disclosure wording and framing, not disclosure per se, determines the cost of AI labels: benefit vs. behavior framing, generic vs. detailed labels, "AI employee" vs. anonymous tool, warmth vs. competence appeals (W7207577485, W7212138832 — Q3 2026; W7222851502, W7223243344 — Q4 2026).
- Proxy metrics routinely decouple from outcomes: CTR vs. completion, clutter-driven gaze vs. recall, watch time vs. sales, reach vs. applicants, impressions vs. sponsor recall (W7214195424, W7222550305, W7222936501, W7223181558, W7223185198).
- Synthetic/virtual representation of marginalized identities backfires without a credible fit signal — disability avatars, AI-generated models of color (W7202162669, W7223056505).
- Congruence and processing fluency recur as the creative mechanism: metaphor, mascot, music/color pairing, appeal–product–character fit in AI video (W7204599802, W7213228572, W7221595688).
- Green and purpose messaging works mainly on audiences already predisposed; admiration and efficacy beliefs, not demographics, predict response (W7203716020, W7220917637, W7223212068).

## Emerging threads
- AI disclosure penalties re-emerging after a neutral/positive phase — attitudes, perceived creativity, cause participation, emotional override — suggesting category and appeal type moderate heavily (W7222851502, W7223070691, W7223243344, W7223056505 vs. earlier W7207577485, W7211870608).
- AI familiarity collapsing as a moderator: heavy users no more sophisticated, familiarity no moderating effect (W7221622840, W7213274355).
- Persuasion knowledge not visible in low-involvement feed behavior — a null that challenges ad-literacy policy (W7221986862).
- Values- and geography-based segmentation displacing demographic segmentation (W7223013238, W7222466110).
- Advertising business model itself as persuasive object: ad-funded discount legitimacy amid retail media and ad-supported tiers (W7222624740).
- Privacy knowledge bifurcating audiences into blockers and pragmatic acceptors rather than producing uniform resistance (W7222483887).
- Nonlinear, ML-derived media-planning effects — step-like frequency, production-cost reversal (W7223172682); watch for replication outside one firm/month.
- Dormant: direct measurement of children's/youth ad exposure, strong in Q3 2026 (W7214801403, W7203907887, W7214311385), absent this run.

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-25: "Consumers aren't rejecting AI ads — they're rejecting what they imply" — disclosure vs. plausibility.
- 2026-10-09: "Children recorded their own phones" — measured child/youth exposure, opt-in age gating, attention engineering, gambling normalization.
- 2026-10-23: "More people watched. Fewer people bought." — attention/engagement metrics running opposite to recall and sales across clutter, creator commerce, sponsorship and dark social (W7222550305, W7222936501, W7223185198, W7223181558, W7223172682).
