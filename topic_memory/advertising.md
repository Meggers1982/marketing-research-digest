# Topic Memory: Advertising
_Last updated: 2026-10-09_

## Established findings
- AI authorship disclosure is not an automatic penalty and is often neutral or positive; what consumers punish is implausibility, perceived corporate intent, or being caught undisclosed (W7204147563, W7214074450, W7203605245 — 2026 Q3; W7207577485, W7211870608, W7214625312 — 2026 Q3/Q4).
- Congruence and processing fluency are a recurring mechanism across creative elements: visual metaphor, mascot style/appeal, music tonality/color saturation (W7204599802, W7204255947, W7213228572).
- Proxy metrics (clicks, reach, recall, engagement) routinely decouple from outcomes: branded search, text-heavy creative, CTR vs completion, admiration vs green behavior (W7204491303, W7212195252, W7214195424, W7203716020).
- Virtual/synthetic endorsers work only under conditions; "diversity" and sensory category are separate design bets, not uniform wins (W7213402036, W7202162669).
- Assertive/commanding copy triggers reactance and slightly reduces persuasion, worst for nonprofits (W7214238772).
- Sponsorship disclosure is an engagement asset, not a cost; covert sponsorship underperforms most for mega-influencers (W7213758328).

## Emerging threads
- Direct measurement of children's and youth ad exposure — child-captured in-game logs, default-setting tobacco ads on streaming, motion as attention engineering, gambling normalization (W7214801403, W7203907887, W7214311385, W7207692856, W7116214838 — 2026 Q3/Q4).
- Disclosure research moving from "does it hurt?" to wording, modality and segment: benefit vs behavior framing, generic vs detailed labels, text vs image, AI-literacy moderation (W7207577485, W7212138832, W7211870608).
- Organizational response to generative AI inside agencies: skilling/reskilling/"deep skilling," and AI authority calibrated to perceived client regulatory culture (W7204571530, W7213885424).
- Capital-markets and governance consequences of marketing spend: abrupt ad cuts raise perceived equity risk; family firms cut ads first under pressure (W7214298879, W7212229180).
- Social proof as conditional rather than universal — large interactor counts lift functional brands, depress symbolic ones (W7220966006); may explain weak average norm effects (W7204247420).
- Immersive formats suppressing trust scrutiny in peer-to-peer markets (W7204201453) — watch for replication beyond lab/approach-tendency outcomes.
- Economic precarity reshaping luxury cues: wealth-coded repels, craft-coded holds (W7213527998).
