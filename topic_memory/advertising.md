# Topic Memory: Advertising
_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

## Established findings
- Fit/congruence, not execution quality, decides creative performance: appeal–product–character fit, humor × brand tier, pride × self-construal, influencer tier × message construal, layout density × positioning, background × product type (W7221595688, W7222466110 — Q4 2026; W7225085686, W7225225862, W7215042802, W7212718074, W7213441852 — Nov 2026).
- Proxy metrics decouple from outcomes, and the problem now extends to causal identification itself: clutter-gaze vs. recall, watch time vs. sales, impressions vs. sponsor recall, reach vs. conversions, and auction-driven overlap violations in observational lift (W7222550305, W7222936501, W7223185198, W7223181558, W4386473346).
- Disclosure wording, not disclosure per se, sets the cost — AI "employee" framing, warmth vs. competence appeals, AI- vs. consumer-subject grammar, mechanism-level targeting notices (W7222851502, W7223243344, W7224590363, W7225262891).
- Purpose and green messaging depends on conditions outside the ad: prior efficacy beliefs, verifiable practice, communications spend, and category visibility (W7220917637, W7223212068, W7223183773, W7213545149).
- Synthetic/performative representation of marginalized identities carries hidden risk, but consumers are unreliable detectors — press scrutiny, not audience vigilance, is the check (W7202162669, W7223056505, W7224213924).

## Emerging threads
- Published nulls and candid small effects on "upgrade" techniques: metaphor vs. plain language, ASMR responsiveness, passive AR/VR exposure, persuasion knowledge in feed browsing (W7225346645, W7223005849, W7225266445, W7221986862).
- Transparency about money reassures; transparency about data plumbing alarms (W7222624740 vs. W7225262891, W7222483887).
- Threat-based appeals underperforming in both commercial and public-health contexts — regret backfires on high-risk perceivers, severity has no direct effect on young audiences (W7213970602, W7212126619).
- Media-planning econometrics getting hard numbers: continuity/dark-quarter share loss, step-like frequency returns, production-cost reversal (W7139097509, W7223172682).
- Location-targeting economics as a new strand — fence placement/size and price set jointly, non-storefront anchors (W7214312085); no empirical test yet.
- AI-authored-creative research, dominant in Oct 2026, nearly absent in Nov 2026 — watch whether the wave has crested or moved journals.
- Dormant: direct measurement of children's/youth ad exposure (W7214801403, W7203907887, W7214311385), absent two runs running.
