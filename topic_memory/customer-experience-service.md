# Topic Memory: Customer Experience & Service
_Last updated: 2026-10-01_

## Established findings
- Anthropomorphism is a liability at failure: human-like robots and avatars are punished harder than plain interfaces, scaling with severity (W7203488123 2026-08-13; W7204275888 2026-08-26; W7203866299 2026-08-21 extends to bystanders).
- Competence, not warmth, drives post-failure outcomes in AI service; emotional/empathetic cues deliver nothing or backfire (W7203609438 2026-08-17; W7203477409 2026-08-14; W7214318124 2026-09-25).
- AI service value depends on authority and governance, not machine-vs-human identity: empowered bots outperform powerless ones (W7203673228 2026-08-18); outcome-based employee empowerment protects against bad AI advice (W7214671269 2026-09-28); visible human oversight protects trust (W7171856573 2026-07-31).
- Recovery should be triaged by severity and remedy type: bots for minor/psychological remedies, humans for compensation and complex or severe failures (W7201883691 2026-08-07; W7203609438 2026-08-17).
- Customer feedback is systematically biased by silence, self-image and language access (W7171377067 2026-07-27; W7202198898 2026-08-11; W7203645795 2026-08-17; W7206172982 2026-09-02); review-mined service quality shares the same self-selection limits (W7202253581 2026-08-12).
- Frontline staff are the gating variable in service tech adoption and outcomes (W7168115520 2026-08-18; W7203840311 2026-08-20; W7214671269 2026-09-28).

## Emerging threads
- Transparency has limits and can backfire: proactive delay notices raise retailer blame (W7172357977 2026-08-03); tracking detail gives least lift to time-pressed shoppers (W7212038717 2026-09-09) — tension with webcare/disclosure findings (W7207820376, W7213292238).
- Attribution theory consolidating as the organizing lens for service failure blame: ideology (W7202074508), AI-agency framing and apology superiority (W7172201743 2026-08-02), communicator blame (W7172357977).
- Service exclusion and capability inequality: self-checkout efficiency paradox (W7197051994 2026-08-06) and dignity in disabled customers' encounters (W7204466776 2026-08-26) — watch whether quantitative validation follows.
- Crisis diffusion driven by high-involvement customers rather than high-follower accounts (W7213665568 2026-09-19) — needs non-Weibo replication.
- AI moving into back-office service design (complaint coding, training recommendations) with relational repair as the weak spot (W7214601318 2026-09-27).
- Brand extensions as service risk: "reversed conspicuousness" in luxury hospitality (W7214625896 2026-09-28) — single qualitative study so far.
- Counter-thread to watch: cheap relational tweaks (user-named bots W7202216162, child-like voices W7203856104) now contradicted by empathy-backfire and anthropomorphism-type evidence; likely moderated by hedonic vs utilitarian context and resolution capability.
