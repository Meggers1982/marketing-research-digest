# Topic Memory: Customer Experience & Service
_Last updated: 2026-11-12_

## Established findings
- Anthropomorphic and lifelike service interfaces are weak or negative levers, punished hardest at failure and extending to bystanders (W7203488123; W7204275888; W7203866299; W7220660112 2026-10-05); the penalty attaches to simulated humanity, not immersive environments, which test positively (W7225124455, W7224128805, W7223701714, 2026-10/11).
- AI and agent service works through competence, authority, fairness and governance rather than warmth claims or machine-vs-human identity (W7203673228; W7214671269; W7214318124; W7221491089 2026-11-01).
- Recovery should be triaged, not standardized — by failure severity, remedy type, social context and loyalty tier (W7201883691; W7203609438; W7221159140 2026-10-09).
- Frontline staff condition is a gating variable: climate framing, stressor type, abuse exposure and provider exhaustion decide outcomes; vulnerability is two-sided (W7221728768; W7221898122; W7220440663; W7223154744 2026-10-20).
- Customer feedback corpora are the default CX instrument, but machine reading captures explicit complaint content and misses relational/positive nuance (W7222527276; W7220919414; W7204606942; W7224826657 2026-10-29 — dissatisfaction legible in text, satisfaction only in voice).
- Values-based and symbolic positioning raises expectations of category-standard execution rather than substituting for it; usefulness and reliability are the stable loyalty foundation (W7216405737; W7222470326; W7215042510; W7225168433 2026-11-03; W7223831545 2026-10-27).
- Customer feedback is systematically biased by silence, self-image, language access and inertia (W7171377067; W7202198898; W7203645795; W7206172982).

## Emerging threads
- Advocacy metrics decouple from repurchase after recovery, especially for virtue-positioned brands held to a higher bar (W7225114666 2026-11-02) — watch for transaction-data replication.
- Escalation as a staged process: mishandled complaints plus brand social stances as radicalization accelerants (W7224485554 2026-10-29), building on diffusion work (W7206212528; W7213665568; W7221524554).
- Sustainability adoption as logistics rather than persuasion — interconnectivity and hub centrality beat green appeals (W7225757846 2026-11-10).
- Transparency reframed from volume to comprehensibility; ambiguity and comprehension burden actively harm (W7222527276; W7204606942; vs W7172357977; W7212038717).
- Observational/ethnographic CX methods positioned against dashboard and text-corpus defaults (W7213312638 2026-09-16).
- Market structure as a CX variable: common institutional ownership tracks with worse service and higher prices (W7223706258) — watch for replication outside airlines.
