## Emerging threads
- AI/robot service outcomes hinge on authority and framing, not machine-vs-human identity: empowered GenAI complaint agents outperform powerless ones (W7203673228, 2026-08-18); AI-as-physician-copilot protects trust while autonomous AI erodes it (W7171856573, 2026-07-31).
- Anthropomorphism is severity-contingent: human-like cues help recovery for mild failures and backfire for severe ones (W7203488123, 2026-08-13); robot-only apologies fit minor failures, human-robot pairs fit serious ones (W7201883691, 2026-08-07).
- Cheap relational design tweaks buffer bot failure: user-chosen chatbot names raise post-failure reuse (W7202216162, 2026-08-11); child-like robot voices raise perceived service quality while vocal gender does not (W7203856104, 2026-08-20).
- Customer feedback is systematically biased: co-creation involvement inflates positive WOM after failure (W7171377067, 2026-07-27); silent customers disclose more to bots with self-benefit framing (W7202198898, 2026-08-11); ESL consumers under-complain absent AI drafting help (W7203645795, 2026-08-17).
- Retention can mask dissatisfaction — loan declines cut satisfaction with little loyalty effect (W7206172982, 2026-09-02).
- Webcare quality and selectivity may beat coverage-rate KPIs (W7207820376, 2026-09-02).
- Frontline employees as the gating variable in service tech: dual adoption (staff then customer) (W7168115520, 2026-08-18); robots seen as supportive coworkers trigger job crafting (W7203840311, 2026-08-20); digital customer demands strain reps in an inverted-U (W7204562935, 2026-08-28).

- Distribution-based CLV (targeting uncertain/"bimodal" customers) outperforms expected-value ranking (W7211878886, 2026-09-07).
- Staff-as-influencer retail models emerging in Japanese fashion (W7213259568, 2026-09-15) — watch for Western retail parallels and labor questions.
