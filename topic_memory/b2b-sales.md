# Topic Memory: B2B & Sales
_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

## Established findings
- Trust is the load-bearing mechanism in B2B relationships, and competence/credibility perceptions matter at least as much as warmth: consultative selling and CSR both work through perceived benevolence plus expertise (W7172448511, 2026-08; W7203828784, 2026-08). Caution: both use 205 matched supplier-buyer pairs and may not be independent.
- Formal control backfires where trust is already strained; relational investment is the stronger lever in channels and sales management (W7208824278, 2026-09; W7211996947, 2026-09; W7203649054, 2026-08).
- Relationship continuity is a performance asset (stable account teams, long-term supplier ties), and long-term ties absorb concrete service failures (W7171153525, 2026-07; W7215060027, 2026-10).
- Dishonesty is less forgivable than failure: distrust and perceived inauthenticity outrank bad experience as drivers of industrial brand avoidance (W7215060027, 2026-10).

## Emerging threads
- Credibility is manufactured, not just demonstrated: metric legitimacy via packaging, enablement and third-party validation (W7214656266, 2026-09); references carrying dysfunctional as well as functional roles (W7212796904, 2026-09).
- ESG as relational and political rather than disclosure problem: procurement tolerance of greenwashing (W7213538226, 2026-09), coalitions pursuing status quo maintenance or market contraction (W7214672998, 2026-09), CSR paying off via buyer trust (W7203828784, 2026-08).
- Employee identification matters, but downstream conversion is weak: LinkedIn ambassador activity barely linked to firm performance (W7213979081, 2026-09) versus prestige-to-performance via identification (W7202160517, 2026-08).
- Models beat rep intuition where explainability is present (W7204437044, 2026-08); conversation tactics are phase-dependent (W7203901346, 2026-08). Watch for replication beyond single firms.
- Buyer-side financial-health scrutiny of suppliers as a brand dimension (W7215060027) — new, worth watching amid insolvency-risk coverage.
- Methodological caution: most B2B work remains cross-sectional, self-report, single-country. Strongest pitches come from matched dyads, time-lagged designs, transaction records and call recordings.

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-21: "The control reflex backfires" — activity monitoring, contract tightening, hard-closing, late-call pressure (W7211996947, W7208824278, W7172448511, W7203901346, W7213538226). HBR, MIT SMR, Marketing Week, Fast Company.
- 2026-10-05: "The machinery of credibility" — buyers reward constructed legitimacy over demonstrated performance; credibility shortcuts losing value (W7215060027, W7214656266, W7203828784, W7214672998, W7213979081, W7212796904). Marketing Week, Digiday, MIT SMR, Raconteur.
- Held for a future run: "data beats gut in B2B" (W7204437044, W7203901346, W7204571119, W7204660594).
