# Topic Memory: B2B & Sales
_Last updated: 2026-11-02_

## Established findings
- Trust and credibility perceptions carry B2B relationships; dishonesty and inauthenticity are punished harder than concrete service failure (W7172448511, 2026-08; W7203828784, 2026-08; W7215060027, 2026-10).
- Relational investment beats formal control where control polices effort (W7208824278, W7211996947, 2026-09) — but formal structure works when it reallocates managerial attention rather than monitoring it (W7224013661, 2026-10).
- Digital capability is a conversion condition for relational and knowledge assets, not a separate budget line (W7203649054, 2026-08; W7223260194, 2026-10).
- Employee and partner advocacy rarely converts downstream: identification drives goal-setting but activity volume barely moves performance, and governance can destroy the authenticity being purchased (W7213979081, 2026-09; W7221433026, 2026-10).

## Emerging threads
- Credibility is manufactured rather than demonstrated — metric legitimation via packaging and third-party validation (W7214656266, 2026-09), disclosure managed by sequencing rather than falsehood (W7224954966, 2026-10).
- Buyer-side meaning-making: committees settle at "interpretive sufficiency," and seller artifacts like references anchor interpretation (W7224794580, 2026-10; W7212796904, 2026-09). Needs quantitative follow-up.
- Sales-workforce capability as the live variable: AI discretion and data-handling gaps (W7221006513), immersive-training abandonment for socio-emotional reasons (W7222981886), late-career inclusion converting only via learning orientation (W7222645606), frontline resistance to DEI programs (W7221916353) — all 2026-10, all small-sample.
- Behavioral call data arrives: acoustic delivery roughly equals semantic content in predicting call outcomes (W7224407372, 2026-10). Watch for replication outside Finland and for implications for conversation-intelligence vendors.
- ESG as relational and political rather than a disclosure problem (W7213538226, W7214672998, 2026-09) — dormant this run, worth re-checking.
- Methodological caution: most B2B work remains cross-sectional, self-report and single-country. Strongest pitches come from matched dyads, quasi-experiments, time-lagged designs and call recordings.
