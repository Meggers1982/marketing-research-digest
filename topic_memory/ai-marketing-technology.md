# Topic Memory: AI & Marketing Technology
_Last updated: 2026-11-09_

## Established findings
- Humanness is a conditional lever and often the wrong dial: over-human bots backfire (W7204492734, W7208749448); anthropomorphism weakens the social-presence-to-satisfaction link in service robots (W7224957158, 2026-10); warmth cues only pay when the bot is disclosed and competent (W7225314486, 2026-11); cheerful tone helps on subjective tasks, hurts on factual ones (W7221784971, 2026-10); stigma contexts may reward less social presence (W7222997881). Unresolved: dominance register (deference wins even in authority contexts, W7224849080, 2026-10) versus emotional valence findings.
- AI disclosure lowers intent through perceived authenticity and effort, consistently across categories: design provenance (W7222531871), souvenirs with market-specific effects (W7223005232), AI-labeled influencers cutting engagement and purchase intent (W7225100007, 2026-11). "Co-created" framing does not reliably rescue it.
- Transparency functions as reassurance rather than caution: adequacy of disclosures strengthened use of risky AI services (W7223088624); clear data-governance copy plus external assurance raised adoption of household low-carbon tech (W7225315132, 2026-11).
- Owning AI is not the payoff — capability, execution and governance convert it: patents pay only via marketing execution (W7222898245); RoAI framework argues delegated judgment erodes the expertise needed to supervise AI (W7223132093, 2026-10); data capability is sequenced practice change (W7213979252).
- AI deployment carries human costs outside its success metric: corner-cutting (W7220733006), misuse intentions rising with felt capability (W7213767799), information avoidance from goal nudges (W7222957362), bystander employee rumination after robot abuse (W7225414092, 2026-11).
- AI-based research instruments need explicit validation: silicon samples compress variance (W7214279921); synthetic LLM respondents require learned credibility weights to match human benchmarks (W4395015967, 2026-10); fluent LLM research output is not the most faithful (W7223791943, 2026-10); conversely LLM+search crawlers beat scanner panels on coverage (W7223205040).
- Trust conversion is gated, not linear: decision confidence is a necessary condition for chatbot quality to convert (W7221013951); explanation volume follows an inverted U with over-justification penalties (W7225383427, 2026-11).

## Emerging threads
- Sophisticated users as the hardest AI audience: technology readiness weakened the trust-to-use link in travel planning (W7224113183, 2026-10), against capability-signal findings on agent delegation (W7222787541).
- AI announcements as a two-audience communications event: abnormal returns up, ESG sentiment down, with responsible-AI orientation blunting the penalty (W7225245070, 2026-11).
- Attributing intent to algorithms: just-below pricing reads as manipulation when AI-set (W7222544706); "people like you" framing triggers identity threat under suspected bias (W7222568097).
- Targeting economics shifting from prediction to persuadability: uplift modeling beat churn-score targeting on real ticketing records (W7223652225, 2026-10).
- User-generated review corpora as continuous, asymmetric quality instruments mapped to ISO quality dimensions (W7224875273, 2026-10).
- Platform ecosystem as a constraint on portability of engagement mechanics (W7222464186); purchasing role as an untracked creative variable (W7221777092).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-14: "The anthropomorphism ceiling" — warmth in AI interfaces stops paying; tune humanness by task.
- 2026-09-28: "The review economy" — the constraint moved from generation to verification; the human check is unbudgeted.
- 2026-10-12: "AI lifts the floor and lowers the ceiling" — expert and senior workers bear the cost of universal AI deployment.
- 2026-10-26: "Disclosure doesn't do what regulators think" — label wording sets price and trust, and warnings read as reassurance (W7222531871, W7223005232, W7222544706, W7223088624).
- 2026-11-09: "The machine-generated consumer" — synthetic respondents and AI-drafted insight entering research workflows without calibration or governance, while the judgment to audit them atrophies (W4395015967, W7223791943, W7223132093, W7224875273).
