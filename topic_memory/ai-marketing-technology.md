# Topic Memory: AI & Marketing Technology
_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

## Established findings
- Humanness and warmth in AI are conditional levers, not defaults: over-human bots backfire (W7204492734, W7208749448); robot empathy helps except in privacy-sensitive settings (W7215001822, 2026-10); chatbot warmth pays on subjective tasks and hurts on factual ones (W7221784971, 2026-10); script tone should match product type (W7219885583). Open dispute: stigma contexts may reward *less* social presence (W7222997881, 2026-10) while anthropomorphism still predicts use (W7223088624).
- Transparency and personalization cues flip sign with salience and sensitivity: privacy notices help only when targeting is vague (W7214342499); warmth reads as intrusion around sensitive data (W7215001822); personalization raises disclosure only for low-sensitivity conditions (W7223240864, 2026-10).
- Possessing AI is not the payoff — execution, skills, review and integration are: patents convert to customer/brand value only via marketing execution capability (W7222898245, 2026-10); data capability is sequenced practice change, not a purchase (W7213979252); Copilot raised output but lengthened review (W4400488941).
- AI acceptance is multidimensional and now measurable (CAAI, W7220664143, 2026-10), with decision confidence a necessary condition for chatbot quality to convert into usage share or lock-in (W7221013951, 2026-10).
- Research and tracking instruments need validation: silicon samples compress variance (W7214279921); sentiment conclusions shift with lexicon choice (W7202341073); conversely, LLM+search crawlers surface 5x more online-native brands than Nielsen or surveys (W7223205040, 2026-10).
- AI tools carry behavioral side effects that output metrics miss: corner-cutting at moderate use (W7220733006); misuse intentions rising with felt capability (W7213767799, 2026-09); information avoidance after motivational goal nudges (W7222957362, 2026-10).

## Emerging threads
- Disclosure wording as a revenue and policy variable: "designed by" vs "designed with" moves WTP and the penalty is largest when AI's hand is visible (W7222531871, 2026-10); "co-created" labels fail to rescue intent and the penalty is market-specific (W7223005232, 2026-10); disclosures act as confidence cues rather than brakes (W7223088624, 2026-10).
- Attributing intent to algorithms: just-below pricing reads as manipulation when AI-set (W7222544706, 2026-10); "people like you" framing triggers identity threat under suspected bias (W7222568097, 2026-10).
- From recommendation to delegation: retained override unlocks acceptance of autonomy in high-threat categories (W7223130166, 2026-10); demonstrable goal-preservation and stated limits raise authorization rates (W7222787541, 2026-10); recommender autonomy must match message frame, with explanations restoring control (W7221585809, 2026-10).
- AI as market infrastructure and regulated terrain: national AI climates (W7220834813), three-tier EU governance with Analytical vs Decisional split (W7221335140), and now evidence that engagement mechanics are platform-ecosystem-bound and non-portable (W7222464186, 2026-10).
- Expert-tier work degrades more than novice work under universal AI deployment (W7220733006, W7214651072, W7220835940, W7204089014) — still unresolved against W4400488941.
- Purchasing role (proxy vs self-buyer) as an untracked creative variable in recommendation imagery (W7221777092, 2026-10).

## Feature ideas already pitched

- 2026-09-28: "The review economy" — the constraint moved from generation to verification, and the human check is unbudgeted.
