# Topic Memory: Consumer Psychology
_Last updated: 2026-11-05_

## Established findings
- Interface architecture is a persuasion variable, now with field evidence: accept-vs-reject framing (W7128495320, 2026-09); review clustering inflating perceived prevalence, stronger on mobile (W7224935301, 2026-10); a single "swap and save" prompt resetting repurchase defaults for ~10 trips via discovery, not savings (W4406862001, 2026-11).
- AI assistance is conditional on category, not universally welcome: delegation helps experiential and frustrates material purchases (W7220380523, 2026-10); text beats voice for search goods, voice wins for experience goods and distracted shoppers (W7223653038, 2026-10); earlier trait-level conditionality (W7204588967, W7204561075, 2026-08).
- Quality and sustainability badges are non-portable: certification credibility stable across segments but framing is not (W7215077900, 2026-10); certification beats origin storytelling in producing countries (W7214722003, 2026-09); eco-cues move food but not shoes (W7222938044, 2026-10); domestic-origin claims beat award badges (W7225201218, 2026-11).
- Gender-blind models keep failing replication: COM-B diet indicators differ for men (W7214384564, 2026-09); body-positive casting lifts women and backfires with men (W7224022072, 2026-10); gendered loneliness-to-impulse pathways (W7225196252, 2026-11).
- Consumption as emotional-state repair, not demographic taste: nostalgia from busyness and fragmented work identity (W7203605101, W7220388832); loneliness driving impulsive buying (W7225196252) and preference for complex, "protective" packaging (W7221515577, 2026-10).
- Visual fidelity does not persuade, but visual complexity sometimes does — AR vividness inert (W7219780211), redundant photos suppress bookings (W7213493969), versus multicolor cool logos arousing via curiosity (W7223786954, 2026-10). Distinction still untested in field.

## Emerging threads
- Vulnerable-state monetization: AI avatars converting anxious buyers by removing social evaluation on low-risk goods (W7224077478, 2026-10), alongside loneliness-impulse evidence — a live consumer-protection storyline.
- Audience counter-surveillance of synthetic media: "crowd forensics" in comment sections, with disclosure inviting scrutiny rather than defusing it (W7225171472, 2026-11); builds on seller identity resets (W7215041317, 2026-10).
- Macro perception as demand driver: perceived inequality raising self-improvement demand via status ambition, with anxiety and envy ruled out (W7224997542, 2026-10).
- Anti-urgency evidence accumulating: interaction-led livestream scripts beating hooks for symbolic goods (W7225239935, 2026-11) plus JOMO appeals (W7214744519), still against FOMO amplification in night-time spending (W7203639635).
- Crisis repair as obligation-specific rather than volume-of-positive-content (W7223841933, 2026-10) — new, needs replication outside experimental stimuli.
- Marketing-claim ethics and denominator framing as a reportable genre (W7225152811, 2026-11).
- Standing caveats: heavy reliance on Chinese and single-country online panels; stated intention rather than observed purchase in most experimental work.
