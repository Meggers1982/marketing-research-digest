# Topic Memory: Consumer Psychology
_Last updated: 2026-09-24_

## Established findings
- Framing and presentation of identical information shifts consumer judgment: duration vs. clock-time wording changes perceived wait length (W7213274443, 2026-09); goal-to-ask divisibility changes donation conversion (W7213625881, 2026-09).
- "More stimulus" can suppress action: redundant image galleries reduce experiential bookings (W7213493969, 2026-09); faces in the pre-skip window reduce views for utilitarian products (W7212428236, 2026-09).
- Effort and friction raise perceived authenticity and value: effortful cause events (W7204997257, 2026-09); rejection of substitutional AI among ideal-self strivers (W7204561075, 2026-08).
- AI benefits are segment-conditional, not universal: emotional chatbot warmth only helps communal-norm customers (W7204588967, 2026-08); substitution framing repels self-improvement-motivated buyers (W7204561075).
- Sustainability and food labels are read very differently by socioeconomic segment; "organic" can carry negative utility (W7213546298, 2026-09).

## Emerging threads
- Interface architecture as a driver of post-purchase regret and returns via blocked "choice closure" (W7214126722, 2026-09) -- watch for field replication with observed returns.
- Human mediation legitimizes synthetic actors: virtual influencers gain trust when shown in genuine social ties with real people (W4318015176, 2026-08).
- Implementation-side AI research shifting from consumer acceptance to manager readiness as the bottleneck (W7171543795, 2026-07).
- Demographic stereotypes breaking down in traditionally male-coded categories: pub choice segments (W7172190947, 2026-09); gendered backlash to rivalry sponsorship (W7203929993, 2026-08).
- Cultural orientation (self-transcendence vs. self-enhancement) as a personalization variable for emotional content (W7213511827, 2026-09).
- Methods caution: collection modality (written vs. spoken) alters the emotional signal in verbatim data (W7213621009, 2026-09).
- Nostalgia demand as state-dependent, triggered by felt busyness (W7203605101, 2026-08).
