# Topic Memory: Consumer Psychology
_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

## Established findings
- Framing and presentation of identical information shifts judgment: duration vs. clock-time wording (W7213274443, 2026-09); goal-to-ask divisibility in giving (W7213625881, 2026-09); accept-vs-reject task framing changes decision time and deferral dynamics (W7128495320, 2026-09).
- Visual maximalism underperforms: redundant image galleries suppress experiential bookings (W7213493969, 2026-09); faces in the pre-skip window cut views for utilitarian products (W7212428236, 2026-09); AR vividness adds nothing over non-AR while interactivity and novelty drive value (W7219780211, 2026-10).
- AI benefits are conditional, not universal — by customer trait (W7204588967, W7204561075, 2026-08) and now by purchase type: delegation helps for experiential, frustrates autonomy for material (W7220380523, 2026-10).
- Nostalgia demand is state-dependent, triggered by self-destabilization: felt busyness (W7203605101, 2026-08) and fragmented multi-job identity via low self-concept clarity (W7220388832, 2026-10).
- Sustainability claims are non-substitutable levers and segment-dependent: organic can carry negative utility (W7213546298, 2026-09); certification credibility is consistent across segments while benefit framing is not (W7215077900, 2026-10); certification beats origin storytelling in producing countries (W7214722003, 2026-09).
- Gender-blind and self-reported segmentation keeps failing: COM-B diet model indicators differ for men (W7214384564, 2026-09); pub segments (W7172190947); rivalry-ad backlash (W7203929993); 53.5% dual-motivated green majority misclassified by self-report (W7215077900).

## Emerging threads
- Identity goods economy: virtual status from avatars/badges/gated access spills onto brand status (W7214899977, 2026-09); free-to-play spend driven by aesthetics-as-self-extension, not power or display (W7212753203, 2026-09).
- Non-human/non-brand subjects outperform the obvious human one — pet eaters as naturalness cue (W7220383982), product-as-subject cart copy (W7220393204), virtual influencers in human social ties (W4318015176).
- Platform trust infrastructure under strain: seller identity resets sever reviews from the business (W7215041317, 2026-10); open geodemographic classifications challenging commercial black boxes (W7203765190, 2026-08).
- JOMO/opt-out appeals as a counter to a decade of scarcity marketing (W7214744519, 2026-09), against evidence that FOMO still amplifies real spending (W7203639635, 2026-08) — unresolved tension.
- Public contestation of consumer identity performance in unequal markets (W7214717190, 2026-09).
- Interface architecture as driver of regret, returns and cognitive load (W7214126722, 2026-09; W7128495320, 2026-09) — still awaiting field replication with observed behavior.
- Heavy reliance on Chinese online panels (Credamo and similar) across multiple 2026 consumer-psychology findings; cross-market replication is the standing caveat.
