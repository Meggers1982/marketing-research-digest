# Topic Memory: Money & Spending
_Last updated: 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- Financial anxiety is driven by subjective appraisal and context, not knowledge or income; replicated across US network modeling (W7202221325, 2026-08) and Brazilian survey data (W7213938599, 2026-09). Financial literacy is a weak lever and can even reduce appetite for buffering credit (W7214655781, 2026-09).
- Trust and affect, not features or comprehension, carry perceived financial well-being: app trust gates perceived savings gains (W7202385295, 2026-08); robo-advisor transparency works via psychological comfort, not usefulness (W7204478707, 2026-08).
- Engagement metrics are hygiene, not commercial value: loyalty/revenue runs through emotional content or structured participation, not likes, interaction or trendiness (W7213557087, W7203581812).
- Debt is sorted into mentally separate categories (asset-building vs expense-buffering), which explains windfalls flowing into mortgage/auto credit rather than deleveraging (W7214655781 + W7213276001).
- Experiential/physical retail does not reliably add demand: it can cannibalize own-channel online sales (W7212434558) and only pays off where the experience fits shopper motivational mode (W7215084470).

## Emerging threads
- Pre-loaded demand: saved ads, wish-lists and parked carts are the real conversion assets; "impulse" is a trigger moment, not a discovery moment (W7213285496, W7213342854).
- Social comparison and visible rivalry as a standalone spending driver, separate from parasocial host attachment (W7214649704), alongside spending-as-membership in fandoms (W7203886708).
- Product proliferation as a cost: more accounts/apps associated with higher anxiety (W7213938599) — watch for replication outside Brazil.
- Saving motives are unstable and shifting from bequest to precautionary/self-directed, with high churn (W7215076186) — watch other aging European markets.
- Sustainability labels as risk-communication failures: "green" crypto contains both shock transmitters and receivers (W7203939686).
- Commercial determinants of health in retail care settings; partnership legitimacy as a judged standard (W7211924177).
- Values-vs-placement gap on visual platforms: algorithmic asymmetry amplifies beauty-ideal content, counter-curation barely registers (W7204130857); ethical claims absorbed and neutralized by "cognitive buffering" (W7213342854).
