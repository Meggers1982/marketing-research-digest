# Topic Memory: Money & Spending
_Last updated: 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- Spending responds most reliably to what other people appear to be doing: norm-framed tip prompts outperform reciprocity appeals (W7224889302, 2026-10); peer advice drives crypto ownership while professional advice suppresses it (W7221884200, 2026-10); livestream envy and fandom membership operate as standalone drivers (W7214649704, W7203886708).
- Felt expertise and felt reassurance, not actual knowledge or comprehension, predict financial behavior and well-being: subjective investment confidence blunts professional advice (W7221884200); robo-advisor transparency works via comfort, not usefulness (W7204478707); literacy is a weak lever on anxiety and can reduce appetite for buffering credit (W7202221325, W7214655781, W7213938599).
- Engagement and exposure are hygiene, not value: likes/trendiness don't drive loyalty (W7213557087, W7203581812); algorithmic exposure drives clicks but conversion depends on self-controlled content and social proof (W7223381933, 2026-10).
- Experiential/physical retail is not reliably additive — it cannibalizes (W7212434558), pays off only on motivational fit (W7215084470), and reorders trips via activity sequence rather than adding footfall (W7221500893, 2026-10).
- Debt is sorted into mentally separate categories (asset-building vs expense-buffering), explaining windfalls flowing into mortgage/auto credit (W7214655781 + W7213276001).

## Emerging threads
- Pre-loaded demand and the discovery/conversion split: wish-lists, saved ads, listing assets are the conversion machinery, not discovery (W7213285496, W7213342854, W7223381933).
- Monetized intimacy without purchase influence: AI companions show heavy in-app spending but ~0.3% commerce involvement — a counterpoint to parasocial-attachment-drives-buying (W7223447670 vs W7214649704). Watch whether companion apps bolt on commerce.
- Pricing psychology beyond affect: token prices beating "free," with delayed reference-price erosion (W7223666241) — watch for replication in real retail and for token-size effects.
- Institutional money as a spending beat: public procurement concentration and electoral churn in large contracts (W7222602776); security vs digitalization budget competition in SMEs (W7220422914).
- Saving motives unstable, shifting bequest-to-precautionary (W7215076186); product proliferation (more accounts/apps) linked to higher anxiety (W7213938599) — both await replication.
- Health/consumption economics with per-person price tags for behavior change (W7223008621).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-20: "The financial-literacy consensus is collapsing" — cash transfers increasing debt, anxiety as a system, trust-gated savings apps (W7213276001, W7202221325, W7202385295). MIT SMR, American Banker, Marketing Week, Vox.
- 2026-10-04: "Feeling right beats understanding" — affective fit, not comprehension or features, predicts spend and well-being (W7204478707, W7215084470, W7203581812, W7214649704, W7213938599). Fast Company, Retail Dive, Tearsheet, The Guardian.
- 2026-11-01: "The trust hierarchy of spending" — peers close sales, algorithms only open the shop window, professionals lose the self-described experts, AI companions monetize intimacy but not recommendations (W7223381933, W7221884200, W7224889302, W7223447670). Digiday, Marketing Brew, MIT SMR, Wired.
