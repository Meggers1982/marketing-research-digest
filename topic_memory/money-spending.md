## Emerging threads
- Financial literacy is a weak lever: it trims extreme anxiety but contextual stressors dominate (W7202221325, 2026-08); institutional communication underperforms creator/peer media at life transitions (W7172533758, 2026-08).
- Windfalls can increase rather than reduce debt, acting as down payments on financed big-ticket purchases (W7213276001, 2026-09) — watch whether other guaranteed-income pilots replicate.
- Trust and framing beat feature design in money tools: app trust gates perceived savings gains (W7202385295); abstract construal dissolves mental earmarks (W7203466714).
- Financial strain redirects rather than suppresses desire — escapist travel intent rises as perceived well-being falls (W7202404765).
- Spending as participation ritual: fan money (K-pop group buys, W7203886708) and structured club participation mechanics (W7213557087) convert identity/engagement into revenue better than creative or social metrics alone.
- Channel substitution, not halo: pop-up/experiential retail cannibalized the brand's own local online sales (W7212434558).
- Long-held heuristics under revision: 80/20 concentration is closer to 20/50 at category level, with a proposed 10/40 yardstick (W7208819722).
- Algorithmic asymmetry on visual platforms — beauty-ideal content amplifies fast, counter-curation barely registers (W7204130857) — a values-vs-placement problem for brands.
- Measurement blind spots: suppressed complaints from solo women travelers mean operator complaint data understates lost revenue (W7172243346).
- Macro-to-consumer linkage: weather shocks in crop regions as leading indicators of FX and household spending power in commodity economies (W7203697215).
- Ad creative designed for saving/screenshotting as an emerging engagement lever, so far evidenced mainly by intentions (W7213285496).
