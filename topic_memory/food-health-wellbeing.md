# Topic Memory: Food, Health & Wellbeing
_Last updated: 2026-11-14_

## Established findings
- Knowledge and information rarely change food behavior; effort, friction, price, taste and emotion do — and label-reading can even track with worse outcomes (W7223125428, W7220842762, W7224129116 2026-10; W7225723597 2026-10).
- Perceived naturalness is the dominant food heuristic, and shoppers compute it crudely: ingredient count (not additives) drives both naturalness and "processed" judgments (W7162453624 2026-10), it outranks meat-mimicry in alternatives (W7223189021), and it splits "food-first" from "medicinal" supplements (W7222525261 2026-10).
- Labels work by redrawing perceived choice sets, not by informing, and they saturate — 42% of shoppers default to a single cue, usually price (W7216368733, W7224129116 2026-10).
- Cheap sensory and design cues reliably move perception: name warmth (W7214622498), package color (W7215082714), hand-feel (W7224004846), plain packs (W7224090846), anthropomorphism lifting perceived quality on upcycled food (W7226018155 2026-11).
- Standard proxies and biomarkers keep failing: rating frequency alters experience (W7214937598), biometric attention predicts nothing without emotional engagement (W7223183113), self-reported waste diverges from weighed waste (W7220842762), PROP supertaster status predicts no alcohol preference (W7223553671 2026-10).
- Credibility and verification beat creative execution in low-trust categories: premium cues can't buy organic claim credibility (W7224137779), trust outmoved gain/loss framing in adoption agriculture (W7225611263 2026-11).
- Health and virtue cues function as permission and guilt relief rather than nutrition information (W7208834717; W7221783757 2026-10).

## Emerging threads
- Nicotine research moving demand-side: from prevalence to intensity (W7223008126), to pre-initiation marketing exposure (W7224916875), to device interface design as an engagement engine (W7225230645 2026-11), with enforcement/illicit-supply modeling now the policy frontier (W7154486028 2026-10).
- Lifecourse extremes entering the topic: avoidant feeding set in month one (W7224627412) and oral capability screening plus preventive-nutrition refusal among older adults (W7225292747, W7222525261 2026-10/11).
- Anti-consumption's class boundary: reflective mortality and mindfulness push toward buying less (W7223010138, W7220360145), but cutting back erodes wellbeing for low-income households (W7222315143 2026-10), and hybrid-product buying raises wellbeing (W7225801646 2026-11).
- Connected hardware sold as wellbeing: smart gym kit works via perceived value and brand love, not directly (W7221082334 2026-10) — same feature set regulators are citing in vapes.
- Store social climate and need states as under-used levers: bystander-observed customer kindness tied to trust and commitment (W7225340314 2026-11); unmet needs trigger concrete construal that inflates perceived value (W7222560284 2026-10).
- GLP-1 consumers remain a distinct, under-measured segment (W7213351764, W7214039691 2026-09); well-powered nulls entering behavior-change tech (W7223232846 2026-10).
