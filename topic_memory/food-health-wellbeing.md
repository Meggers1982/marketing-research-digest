# Topic Memory: Food, Health & Wellbeing
_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

## Established findings
- Information and supply alone don't shift eating; repeated exposure does not breed liking — users develop workarounds instead (W7202165920 2026-08; W7213326632 2026-09; W7206215832 2026-09; W7210275208 2026-09).
- Labels and terminology redraw mental category boundaries rather than merely informing: "cell-based" carves out a novelty category (W7211919306 2026-09), extended welfare labels enlarge the perceived animal-friendly choice set and lift plant-based choice ~28% (W7216368733 2026-10).
- Cheap design and sensory cues reliably move choice, each with a named mechanism: plate shape/fluency (W7212607085), nostalgic packaging/perceived effort (W7204661572), brand-name length/warmth (W7214622498), package color/pester power (W7215082714).
- GLP-1 consumers are a distinct segment whose shifts in taste and cue reactivity sit below self-report, and the field still lacks a validated measure (W7213351764 2026-09; W7214039691 2026-09).
- Virtue and health cues can backfire: "high protein" reads as processed (W7208834717); green delivery licenses indulgence (W7172408085); explicit health framing deters hard-to-reach men (W7213513131).
- For synthetic personas, trust, disclosure and audience literacy beat realism; photorealism shows an uncanny-valley penalty (W7207770543 2026-09, building on W7212302631, W7170143709, W7171940513).

## Emerging threads
- Measurement artifacts: rating frequency alters the experience (W7214937598), repeat use masks dislike (W7210275208), review silence is a weak signal (W7215031814), awareness and favorability diverge (W7214439490).
- Regulated-category channel migration and industry conduct — tobacco as template: supermarket exit to tobacconists/informal sellers (W7214949285), subsidy dependency (W7214928046), internal documents on illicit trade (W7215133135).
- Consumer and seller vulnerability as a marketing-journal staple — subsistence vendor dignity (W7214083501) alongside algorithmic financial exclusion (W7170190396, W7203676845).
- Caregiver behavior as the under-used lever in child feeding: modeling eating (W7213662492); parental nutrition knowledge as moderator of packaging effects (W7215082714).
- Emotion-based routes to food-waste reduction — other-directed anger (W7208792358) alongside design cues (W7204661572).
- Wellbeing without autonomy: micro-level playful framing cutting same-day exhaustion in rigid roles (W7214901172).

## Feature ideas already pitched

- 2026-10-03: "Your satisfaction data isn't measuring satisfaction" — ratings, repeat use, review silence and awareness as broken proxies (W7214937598, W7210275208, W7215031814, W7214439490). HBR, MIT SMR, Marketing Week, Skift.
