# Topic Memory: Food, Health & Wellbeing
_Last updated: 2026-10-31_

## Established findings
- Knowledge and information rarely change food behavior; effort, friction, price, taste and emotion do (W7202165920 2026-08; W7210275208 2026-09; W7223125428 2026-10; W7220842762 2026-10; W7224129116 2026-10).
- Labels work by redrawing perceived choice sets, not by informing — but they saturate: extending welfare labels lifted plant-based choice ~28% (W7216368733 2026-10), while 42% of shoppers default to a single cue, usually price, when labels stack (W7224129116 2026-10).
- Cheap sensory and design cues reliably move perception and choice, each with a named mechanism: name length/warmth (W7214622498), package color/pester power (W7215082714), hand-feel/sensation transference (W7224004846), plain packs/appeal (W7224090846).
- Standard marketing proxies measure the wrong thing: rating frequency alters experience (W7214937598), repeat use masks dislike (W7210275208), review silence is weak signal (W7215031814), attention to a label predicts nothing without emotional engagement (W7223183113), self-reported waste diverges from weighed waste (W7220842762).
- Virtue and health cues function as permission and guilt relief rather than nutrition information, and can repel non-health-focused segments (W7208834717; W7172408085; W7221783757 2026-10).
- GLP-1 consumers remain a distinct, under-measured segment whose cue reactivity shifts below self-report (W7213351764; W7214039691 2026-09).

## Emerging threads
- Green communication saturation: self-flattery suppresses green buying (W7223183113), premium cues can't buy claim credibility (W7224137779), ESG media visibility shows an inverted-U with firm value (W7223696657), and the public misjudges which policies work (W7223275804).
- Tobacco/nicotine research shifting from supply and conduct (W7214949285, W7214928046, W7215133135) to demand intensity and pre-initiation (W7223008126, W7224916875) plus consolidated packaging evidence (W7224090846).
- Infant feeding window moving earlier: avoidant feeding trajectories set in month one and persist (W7224627412), alongside under-used caregiver modeling (W7213662492).
- Wellbeing from micro-episodes rather than job redesign: playful framing in rigid roles (W7214901172), informal episodes and channel choice in hybrid work (W7224750355).
- Anti-consumption and meaning: reflective mortality framing and mindfulness novices shifting toward buying less (W7223010138, W7220360145).
- Physical context as a category driver: crowd-density "spatial compatibility" favoring merchandise over F&B (W7221437963); date labels penalizing pantry goods more than fresh (W7220982864).
- Well-powered nulls entering the literature — food-specific inhibitory control training failed with Bayesian support for the null (W7223232846).
