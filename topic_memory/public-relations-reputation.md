## Emerging threads
- Formal apparatus underperforms visible behavior: compliance leadership mediated 71% of formal governance effects on whistleblowing intentions (W7213244866, 2026-09); AI training showed no effect while organizational AI support did (W7205004055, 2026-09); crisis playbook deviations driven by legal/client pressure, not ignorance (W7203759226, 2026-08).
- CSR and social-impact communication increasingly audited against supply-chain reality — bidi industry CSR atop wages at 38–53% of legal minimum (W7220403023, 2026-10).
- AI is reshaping PR roles toward judgment, interpretation, and ethical stewardship rather than eliminating them (W7213310447, 2026-09); pairs with W7205004055 on governance over individual skill.
- Identity and moral orientation beat information/price as behavior drivers: symbolic (no-discount) promotions lift sales via community rootedness (W7215933723, 2026-10); stigma as informal market regulation blocks menstrual product trial (W7214428578, 2026-09); moral-orientation personas separate persuadable from unreachable opponents (W7214777517, 2026-09).
- Reputation as pre-crisis asset: heritage cues buffer negative publicity via perceived authenticity ("heritage heuristic," W7214178032, 2026-09).
- Geopolitical positioning as reputation decision — full market exit beats partial exit with home-country consumers (W4414312759, 2026-08).
- Brand meaning as distributed/co-created rather than managed: residents' unplanned contributions shape place brands (W7202171291, 2026-08).
- Representation parity does not equal authority in women-majority communications sectors (W7213930759, 2026-09) — watch for replication outside India.
- Non-Western and Global South practitioner evidence (India, UAE, South Africa) appearing more often in PR/comms journals; most findings still single-country and cross-sectional.
