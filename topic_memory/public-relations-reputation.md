# Topic Memory: Public Relations & Reputation
_Last updated: 2026-11-03_

## Established findings
- Information-deficit communication underperforms: barriers to sustainable/stigmatized behavior are structural or social, not knowledge gaps — plastics opportunity constraints and preference for regulation (W7225111854, 2026-11), canteen convenience over eco-concern (W7223028679, 2026-10), stigma as market regulator (W7214428578, 2026-09), moral-orientation personas (W7214777517, 2026-09).
- Brand meaning is co-created by parties the brand does not control: creators legitimated a new sports league (W7225053690, 2026-11); residents author place brands through ordinary life (W7202171291, 2026-08).
- Formal apparatus underperforms visible behavior and real conditions: compliance leadership mediated 71% of governance effects (W7213244866, 2026-09); AI training null vs. organizational support (W7205004055, 2026-09); crisis playbooks diluted by legal/client pressure (W7203759226, 2026-08). Partial counterpoint: perceived access to ethics/equity resources mediated crisis leadership perceptions (W7211909650, 2026-09).
- CSR/purpose communication is routinely audited against underlying conduct and found wanting — bidi CSR atop sub-minimum wages (W7220403023, 2026-10); a century of NFL "racelighting" (W7225126842, 2026-11).

## Emerging threads
- AI in PR driven by competitive compulsion rather than demonstrated results, with governance left to individual discretion (W7222613543, 2026-10); efficiency case weakens sharply in under-resourced languages (W7221812755, 2026-10); builds on role-shift toward ethical stewardship (W7213310447, 2026-09).
- Audience-side response to corporate AI talk is now being measured: instrumental AI messaging draws positive sentiment but less engagement; CSR framing removes the penalty (W7208830817, 2026-09).
- Sustainability commitments as politically and structurally contingent: green packaging retreats under anti-climate populism (W7214508893, 2026-10); Scope 3 emissions structurally unmeasurable and inflated by supplier diversification (W7224156801, 2026-10); green ambition commercially neutral for scaling (W7224854055, 2026-10).
- Message format may beat source identity in CSR persuasion — co-creation cues outperform NGO attribution (W7223321733, 2026-10). Watch for replication outside India/Facebook.
- Epistemic credibility over emotional warmth in contested information environments — neutral factual pharma content outperformed positive framing (W7223996694, 2026-10).
- Distribution and institutional capacity, not certification, as the bottleneck in origin branding ("terroir paradox," W7223808860, 2026-10).
- Reputation as pre-crisis asset (heritage heuristic, W7214178032, 2026-09) and geopolitical positioning as reputation decision (full vs. partial market exit, W4414312759, 2026-08) — neither yet replicated.
- Evidence base remains heavily single-country, cross-sectional, and increasingly non-Western (India, UAE, South Africa, Korea, Arab states, Türkiye, New Zealand).
