# Topic Memory: Sustainability & Ethical Consumption
_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

## Established findings
- Sustainability earns credit when visible, voluntary and singular; invisible, subtractive, mandated or mixed messages earn little or none — and lightweighted packs are actively misread as low-value waste (W7212134127, W7212064195, W7170105320, Sep 2026; W7214074641, Sep 2026).
- Green attributes win stated approval but rarely a price premium; functional trust/safety attributes (reuse, tamper seals) do get paid for (W7204076930, Aug 2026; W7172534656, Aug 2026).
- Environmental motives are seldom the operative driver of "sustainable" purchases: status, brand access, curiosity, security, taste and threshold hygiene factors dominate (W7213254319, W7211939433, W7204951358, Sep 2026; W7213931215, W7214159818, W7214091952, Sep 2026).
- Accurate climate knowledge does not produce acceptance in food; familiarity, satiety and disgust govern (W7211939433, W7214091952).
- Corporate CSR's consumer payoff is indirect (loyalty, perceived ethicality/authenticity) and highly contingent on governance and perceived sincerity (W7212036936, W7212064195, W7213035748, W7214208660).
- Most evidence in this topic remains stated-preference, single-country and intention-based; WTP figures should be treated as hypothetical.

## Emerging threads
- Post-purchase as the real choke point: disposal, retention and resale decisions, driven by security/autonomy values and value cues, not eco-attitudes (W7214074641, W7214159818, W7213931215).
- Authority beats identity: expert endorsement outperformed commitment-consistency appeals and showed no cultural moderation, cutting against "localize everything" advice (W7214115221 vs W7212036936).
- Halo spillover and proxy verification: diversity read as environmental commitment (W7172382856); plastic seals as authenticity proof (W7172534656); sportswashing via athlete philanthropy (W7214088398).
- Attribution split on the attitude–behavior gap: self-blame and identity fatigue (W7211935268) versus structural/system blame among young US shoppers (W7213447462).
- Governance as the limiting factor on ESG substance — investor attention capacity, ESG-linked pay, mandated disclosure (W7214208660, W7213035748, W7212064195).
- Audiences performing public greenwashing audits while separating messenger from cause (W7214725050).
- AI shifting from consumer-facing novelty to creative production infrastructure (W7214980150 after W7213257365, W7213367223).
- Consumer labor and access equity in fragmented services (W7204439549, W7211969452).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-18: "The credit gap" — sustainability rewarded for legibility, not substance; mandated/moralizing claims destroy value (W7212134127, W7204076930, W7212064195, W7170105320). Suggested Marketing Week, MIT SMR, The Grocer, Vox.
- 2026-10-02: "The last mile of the circular economy" — disposal, retention and resale, not purchase, as the failure point; lightweighting backfiring at the curb (W7214074641, W7214159818, W7213931215, W7172534656). Suggested Packaging Dive, Retail Dive, Grist, Marketing Brew.
